Lighthouse Timeline

1972

Congress ordered the U.S. Coast Guard to regulate the Lighthouse. A fully automated airport beacon lens was installed which allowed for seventeen miles of visibility over the surface of Lake Michigan.

1973

The Coast Guard personnel discontinue full time care of the light station.

June 20, 1977

The Coast Guard allowed the State of Michigan, Department of Natural Resources to purchase the property.

October 8, 1987

The Gulliver Historical Society is established and sets its main priority of historically preserving the Seul Choix Point Lighthouse.

August 28, 1988

The lighthouse is added to the State and National Historic Registers.

January 6, 1989

A Michigan Equity Grant program helps pave the way for the Historical Society to establish a Gulliver Historical Museum in the fog signal building. A grand opening celebration is held on August 6, 1989.

1990

The Historical Society begins interior and exterior restoration of the lighthouse. A Gulliver Centennial book was published and celebration held.

1994

A Michigan Coastal Management Grant was obtained. Complete restoration and painting of the exterior lighthouse and tower were completed.

1995

Interior restoration of the living quarters was completed and a huge Lighthouse Centennial celebration was held. A Centennial History book was completed.

1996

A Michigan Coastal Management Grant was obtained and interior painting and restoration of the light tower was completed. Daily guided tower tours were added. A well-stocked gift shop and shirt shop was added.

About Us

The Gulliver Historical Society is dedicated to preserving the maritime history and structures of Seul Choix Point.
